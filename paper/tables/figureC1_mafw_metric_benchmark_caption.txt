Metric-level discrepancies between LOMM and MAFW classifier-score series. Blue points are medians across 100 LOMM simulations. Gray points and bars show the median and central 95% reference range across 1,000 disjoint MAFW-to-MAFW comparisons. Results are averaged across four folds and shown for scores and log-ratio representations. Discrepancy is the larger of the standardized absolute median difference and standardized Wasserstein distance. Smaller values indicate closer agreement. Eight of 22 LOMM medians exceeded the corresponding reference interval. The largest departures concerned sample entropy and spectral entropy in both representations.
