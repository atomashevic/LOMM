Score-scale discrepancy between simulated and real score series for the eight configurations in the five-fold cross-fitted exploratory comparison. Points are mean discrepancies across eight summary statistics; smaller values indicate closer agreement with the real scores from 100 video clips. The diamond marks the clip baseline + scale configuration, and the dotted line marks the common baseline-and-scale configuration.
