Real MAFW classifier scores for a fixed 49-frame clip compared with the closest of 100 LOMM realizations generated with the clip baseline + scale configuration. The realization shown minimized an equally weighted average of the root mean squared error in the scores and in their frame-to-frame changes, each standardized by the corresponding scale in the real data, across all 11 emotions (realization 26; distance = 0.926). Selection used unsmoothed values; the plotted highlighted trajectories use a seven-frame moving average.
